How to move
rail │ projects │ worktrees │ chat │ files │ │ │ ▲ │ │ ⌃H ◀──┼───────────┼──┘ ├──▶ ⌃L │ │ │ ⌃J/⌃K stack

The lane. Panels sit side by side. ⌃H ⌃L cross them, ⌃1–⌃9 jump by position, ⌘W closes one.

The cursor. Inside a panel j k move, g goes to the top, ⏎ opens. Bare letters act on the row under the cursor.

The composer. i to type, esc to leave. Ctrl keys keep working while you type.

When lost. ⌘⇧P lists every command, ⌘K starts a chord, ? opens this.
