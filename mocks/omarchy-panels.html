Floe panels on Omarchy's layout rules

Rules — system-wide when this is implemented every panel, every list, every field

  1. R1 Behaviour is untouched. Layout and skin only. Same panels, same sidebar, same keys, same commands.
  2. R2 Panels sit close, not flush. A 6px gutter between panels and around the lane, each panel with its own 1px --line border. Flush was tried and felt cramped.
  3. R3 No rounded corners. --radius: 0 everywhere: panels, heads, fields, chips, buttons, menus, dialogs, toggles. Dots stay dots.
  4. R4 Rows span the full panel width. Panel bodies carry no horizontal padding. The 12px text inset lives on the row. The cursor band and its 2px accent bar reach the panel's own edge.
  5. R5 Same row everywhere. Sidebar sessions, commands, changed files, settings rows, facts, menu rows: one row height, one inset, one cursor treatment (accent bar + accent text), hover an 8% wash, no fills.
  6. R6 Section headings are caption size, uppercase, muted, 1px letter-spacing, same inset as rows.
  7. R7 The composer is a strip. Full panel width at the bottom of the chat, accent hairline on top when focused, no box inside a box.
  8. R8 Fields and buttons are background inside a hairline; focus turns the hairline accent. Buttons carry their key beside the label.
  9. R9 Focus on a panel is its name in accent and the head hairline in --line. Nothing else moves.
  10. R10 Three text sizes, one mono face: caption 10, body-small 11, body 12.

Floe's panel model stays: a lane of panels, each with its head and body, focus on the panel, the glyph rail on the right. The panels sit flush, separated by hairlines, no gutter, as in the columns frame you picked. No tabs. What comes from Omarchy/Flea is the skin and what lives inside the panels: radius 0, hairlines, uppercase caption headings, the cursor row with the 2px accent bar, the sidebar you have today with its worktree cards, the facts table inside the review panel, and the menu, dialog and settings from F3. Status bar and breadcrumb are the two things you were unsure about, so they are variants below, not defaults.

F1 — no breadcrumb, no status bar panels only

The top bar and the status bar are both gone. Each panel head carries its own context: the worktree card says its name, the chat says the session, the harness and the state, the terminal and commands panels sit beside it as today, the review says the diff size. Usage moved to the composer's meta line, where the model already is. The sidebar is the one Floe has now: one card per worktree with its sessions, ⌘1… to jump, the premise as a caption line under the rows, no SESSIONS label since that is all a card holds, and the new-worktree card filling the rest. Only the skin changed: square, hairline, uppercase labels, the accent bar on the cursor row.

F1b — status bar only Omarchy's one transient line

Same window with Omarchy's status bar at the bottom and nothing on top. It is the one place that talks: counts on the left, the running action or the last error on the right, gone after four seconds, and the key hint. If this reads as noise, F1 is the answer. If it earns its 26px, every notice, update banner and "merge refused" in Floe becomes this line.

F1c — breadcrumb only Floe's current top bar, squared

And the other way round: the top bar Floe has today, kept but flattened into a 30px strip on the surface plane, usage on its right. No status bar. This is the least change from what runs now.

F2 — menu, settings panel and dialog in context from F3

The row menu opens beside the worktree row, its chords in a right column so hints can be switched off without reflow. Settings is a panel in the lane like any other, a rail on its left and label + caption + control rows. The new-worktree dialog is centred over the lane: fields are background inside a hairline, accent when focused, buttons carry their key.

F3 — states focus · cursor · hover · selection · errors

Four states, one rule each. A focused panel gets the --edge border and its name in accent; nothing else moves. The cursor row is the accent bar plus accent text. Hover is an 8% wash. Multi-select marks the glyph in accent and leaves the text alone, so a selection and the cursor can share a row. An error row is text in the error colour, no icon, no fill.
