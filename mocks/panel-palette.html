Panel dialog, typing head — A with P3, B with P4

One surface for both jobs: panel chrome from O1, the head is where you type, the answers stack underneath, the foot names the keys. Switching a project and adding one are then the same box in two states — ⌘K never changes shape under you. A fills the body with grouped sections (P3); B splits it and explains the highlighted row (P4).

A — typing head + grouped body (P3)

The body is a grouped list: recents first, then the groups. Adding a project is the SAME box with its sections turned into the two answers it still needs — machine and group — while the head holds the path you are typing. Nothing moves between the two states except what is under the head.
