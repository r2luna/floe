Files panel — rooting at a folder inside the worktree

Five ways to show “this tree is pointed at src/renderer/src, not at the worktree”, and the way back out. Same rows in each, cursor on Composer.tsx.

O1 — Header breadcrumb

No new chrome: the header sub becomes the path, each segment clickable to climb. Costs nothing in vertical space; the path competes with the panel name when narrow.
