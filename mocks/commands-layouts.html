Floe — the commands pane, five layouts

Same five processes, the app's real stylesheet. Option 0 is what ships today.

Why it reads the way it does. 1. The buttons are opacity: 0 until the pointer is on the row (.cmd-acts, index.css:3326) — they are not hard to see, they are not drawn. Every option below makes them visible and quiet; that is held constant, not varied. 2. A row is two lines with padding: 5px 8px and nothing between rows, so five commands are ten lines in one block — the two-line row has the separation of a one-line list. 3. Three things claim margin-left: auto on the same row: the state on line one, the note on line two, then the actions after it. The right edge is decided per line rather than as a column.
