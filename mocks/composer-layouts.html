Composer — one line, six ways to fit + · mode · send

The tools row is gone: +, the mode chip and send share the text line, and the box is one line tall at rest. Harness, model and effort are dropped — the chat head already says who you are talking to — leaving the mode, which is the only part you change mid-chat. Each layout is shown at rest, mid-sentence, and with an attachment + a wrapped line.

C1 — Attach left, mode and send right

The obvious one: + at the head of the line, the mode and send at the tail. Send is a bare ⏎ hint while the box is empty and becomes the arrow once there is something to send, so nothing on the line is dead weight.

C6 — Send welded to the edge
