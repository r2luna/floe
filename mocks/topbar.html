Top bar: project, branch, and what is left before merge

One 30px strip across the top of the window, above the lane. Left side answers where am I: project, branch, the base it merges into, and the machine when it is not this one. Right side answers what is left: uncommitted files, unpushed commits, and whether the branch is ready to merge. It follows the worktree of the focused panel, so switching chats with ⌘1… updates it. It is also the window's drag handle, which the frameless window does not have today. The numbers are the sidebar's existing + ~ − ⇡ ⇣ counts plus one new one: commits ahead of the base.
