Pasting 900 lines into the composer

Four ways to hide the bulk. The full text always goes out on send — only the reading changes. Click the pills.

O1 Inline token — the paste becomes one word in the sentence

The same trick [image 01] already uses: the textarea holds a short token, the mirror paints it as a pill. It keeps its place in the sentence, so you can write before and after it. Click opens a read-only peek; ⌫ on the token deletes the whole paste.

O2 Attachment chip — the paste leaves the text entirely

Treated like a dropped file: a chip above the bar, nothing in the textarea. The composer stays one clean line. The text is appended to the message on send, so the model still reads it verbatim.

O3 Folded block — the text is all there, just clamped

Nothing is moved or tokenised. The pasted run is drawn as a two-line window with a fade and a counter; click unfolds it to a scrollable 190px and click again refolds. What you see is literally what is in the textarea.

O4 Quote rail — one line of the paste, kept as a quote

Halfway between O1 and O3: the run collapses to a rail with the first line and a count, reading like a quoted block rather than a pill. Click expands in place. Same mechanism as O1 (a token under the hood), a softer look.
